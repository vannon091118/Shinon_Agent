## Engine Baseline (V70)

### Laws & Government
- Law Categories: Civic, Criminal, Military, Economic, Religious
- Law enactment requires Government Points (GP) + building tiers
- Law effects scale with Settlement Level + Culture

### Slavery System (V70 - Pre-Reign of Terror)
- Enslavement via Law + Crime punishment only
- Slave Price fixed per Race (`SLAVE_PRICE` property)
- Slave Trade NOT a diplomatic action
- Slaves = Resource, not Population Class

### Population Growth
- Formula: `BASE * CLIMATE * TERRAIN * HOUSING`
- `POPULATION.GROWTH` stat = multiplier
- Migration separate from natural growth
- No negative growth

### Race Relations
- Static `OTHER_RACES` in Race definition
- Affects Immigration + Trade willingness
- No dynamic events affecting relations

### Tech Tree
- 5 Categories: Civic, Military, Scientific, Religious, Economic
- Costs scale with Era
- No faction-specific trees
