## Engine Baseline (V68)

### Laws & Government
- Basic Law System (Categories only)
- No Government Points system
- Law effects hardcoded, no scaling

### Slavery System
- Enslavement via Court punishment only
- Fixed `SLAVE_PRICE` per Race property
- No Slave Markets, no Raid-based acquisition
- Slaves = Resource subclass

### Population
- Growth Formula: `BASE * CLIMATE * TERRAIN * HOUSING`
- `POPULATION.GROWTH` multiplier
- Immigration + Natural Growth combined

### Race Relations
- Static `OTHER_RACES` in Race file
- No dynamic events

### Tech Tree
- Basic 5 Categories
- Linear progression
- No Era scaling, no Faction-specific trees

## SyxCraft V68 State
